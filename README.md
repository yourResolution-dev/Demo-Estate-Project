# ESTATE & CO. - Premium Real Estate Website Frontend

Bespoke luxury real estate website frontend designed for **ESTATE & CO.** and integrated into the **ReSolution** demo showcase ecosystem.

## Brand & Route
- **Brand**: ESTATE & CO. (PREMIUM REAL ESTATE)
- **Route**: `/demos/real-estate` (and `/` as default fallback)
- **Sub-routes**:
  - `/demos/real-estate/properties`: Full catalog with multi-facet filters & sorting
  - `/demos/real-estate/property/:id`: Individual high-res property detail showcase
  - `/demos/real-estate/projects`: Signature developments and master plans
  - `/demos/real-estate/agents`: Private client property advisors
  - `/demos/real-estate/about`: Heritage, fiduciary values, and 15+ years track record
  - `/demos/real-estate/contact`: Headquarters, confidential inquiry desk, and VIP WhatsApp

## Features
- **Visual Match**: Recreated from the provided ESTATE & CO. visual reference with deep forest/charcoal backgrounds, warm ivory content sections, muted gold accents, serif typography, and architectural photography.
- **Hero & Search Panel**: Interactive Buy/Rent/Invest tabs with live location, property type, budget, and bedroom filtering.
- **Statistics**: 1,200+ Properties Listed, 850+ Happy Clients, 15+ Years Experience, 8+ Prime Locations.
- **Featured Properties**: Realistic INR pricing, specifications (Beds, Baths, Sq.ft), status tags (For Sale, Ready to Move, Under Construction), and wishlist toggling.
- **Why Estate & Co.**: 4 benefit cards (Verified Listings, Investment Guidance, End-to-End Support, Dedicated Experts).
- **Signature Projects**: Interactive horizontal showcase (Estate Greens, Rivermist Heights, Maple Residency) with modal previews.
- **Advisor Profiles**: Advisor cards with rating, client volume, direct phone/WhatsApp actions, and profile modals.
- **Interactive Lightbox**: Fullscreen architectural gallery modal with keyboard navigation.
- **Persistent Wishlist**: Saved properties synchronized to `localStorage` with drawer panel.
- **ReSolution Integration**: Top showcase banner, conversion modal ("Want a Website Like This?"), and direct WhatsApp integration.
